🔄 Circular Flow Detection

Launderers route funds through multiple intermediary shell entities and return them to the origin to create a faux appearance of legitimate commercial trade. FinTrace runs Tarjan's strongly connected components algorithm in NetworkX to uncover cycles in directed transaction graphs.

🌪️ Smurfing / Structuring (Fan-In)

Dozens of individual "smurf" runners deposit amounts just under the $10,000 Currency Transaction Reporting (CTR) threshold into a central collector account. FinTrace detects this via extreme In-Degree to Out-Degree ratios ($k_{in} \gg k_{out}$) within short time windows.

🎯 Betweenness Centrality Hubs

Betweenness Centrality ($C_B(v)$) measures how often an account lies on the shortest path between all other pairs of accounts. High $C_B$ indicates crucial money aggregator hubs and shell company conduits orchestrating syndicate-level transfers.

⚖️ Regulatory Compliance & Anti-Black-Box Mandates

Financial regulators (including RBI in India, FinCEN in the US, and FATF internationally) strictly prohibit "black-box" automated enforcement without human-interpretable justifications. FinTrace embeds TreeSHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) directly into the analyst workflow, converting complex ensemble decision boundaries into mathematically verifiable contribution scores ($\phi_i$) that satisfy full legal scrutiny.
